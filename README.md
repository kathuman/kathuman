## 🚀 Live deployments

### [Estay Dynamics](https://kathuman.github.io/estay-dynamics/) — consulting practice site
System dynamics and network optimization for supply chains, with two interactive research tools built in:

- **[Global Disruption Atlas](https://kathuman.github.io/estay-dynamics/atlas/)** — an interactive 3D globe of a live supply chain network: ports, factories, and corridors; live-style disruption signals (weather, strikes, geopolitical); scenario overlays (reroute, buffer, alternate-source); country borders and names; and CSV import to plot your own network.
- **[DDRA — Dynamic Cyber-Risk Model](https://kathuman.github.io/estay-dynamics/ddra/)** — a five-stock system dynamics model of the attack-defence loop in a cyber-physical ship ballast system. Move the sliders and watch detection delay decide containment vs. failure.

- **[Cobot Lab — Robotic Arm Simulator](https://kathuman.github.io/claude-projects/cobot-lab/)** — 3D simulator of the Universal Robots UR3e/UR5e/UR10e/UR16e cobots, built from their published kinematic and dynamics parameters, or any 6-joint arm imported from a URDF file: analytic and numerical inverse kinematics, Move J / Move L / Move C timed within joint limits, a collision guard, RRT-Connect path planning around obstacles, Rapier physics with a solid arm and real pick & place, motor-load torques, a teach-pendant program editor with URScript export, a ROS bridge, and a guided tutorial at Basic, Intermediate and Advanced levels · part of [Projects Built with Claude](https://github.com/kathuman/claude-projects)
- **[Tube Flow — CFD Sphere-in-a-Tube Sim](https://kathuman.github.io/claude-projects/tube-flow-sim/)** — real-time 3D lattice-Boltzmann Navier–Stokes solver: drag inlet speed and viscosity through Stokes creeping flow, a steady separated wake, and unsteady vortex shedding, with a live Reynolds number, thousands of advected tracer particles, and an optional speed/vorticity cross-section slice · part of [Projects Built with Claude](https://github.com/kathuman/claude-projects)
- **[Warehouse Model — Interactive Engineering Reasoning Environment](https://kathuman.github.io/claude-projects/warehouse-model/web/)** — warehouse design from first principles: a parametric FreeCAD model and an analytical model share one layout (a parity test proves they agree), with five rack types, fire-code and sprinkler checks, exact lift-truck cycle times, ABC slotting, I-/U-flow docks and Erlang C truck queues; a detailed live 3D view with a travel-time heat map; a discrete-event simulation of the day with Monte Carlo ranges; total cost of ownership, scenarios, sensitivity analysis and an optimiser for the cheapest design meeting the targets; import of real movement and inventory data; and export to IFC (BIM), DXF and glTF · part of [Projects Built with Claude](https://github.com/kathuman/claude-projects)
